# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Insights
1. **Healthy growth.** Sales grew from $484K (2015) to $733K (2018), up 51%. Profit almost doubled from $49.5K to $93.4K, so the business is scaling, not just selling more.
2. **Furniture sells a lot but earns almost nothing.** It is 32% of sales ($742K) but only $18.5K of profit (2.5% margin). Technology and Office Supplies both earn about 17%.
3. **Tables, Bookcases and Supplies lose money.** Tables alone lost $17.7K on $207K of sales (-8.6% margin).
4. **Discounts are the biggest profit leak.** Lines with no discount earned $321K. Lines discounted 40%+ lost $99.6K (933 orders, avg -$107 each). Discount and margin are strongly negatively correlated (-0.86).
5. **Central region is the weak spot.** Margin is 7.9% against 14.9% in the West and 13.5% in the East. Texas (-$25.7K), Ohio (-$17.0K), Pennsylvania (-$15.6K) and Illinois (-$12.6K) are the loss-making states.
6. **Small items are quiet winners.** Paper (43%), Labels (44%), Envelopes (42%) and Copiers (37%, $55.6K profit) have the best margins.
7. **Consumers drive volume.** The Consumer segment brings 51% of sales; Corporate and Home Office both earn a similar margin of about 13-14%.
8. **1 in 5 order lines loses money** (18.7% of all lines), and the heavy losses cluster in high-discount big-ticket items such as 3D printers and binding machines.

## Recommendations
1. **Cap discounts at 20-30% and require approval above that.** Lines discounted 30%+ lost $135K in total. Even breaking even on them would lift total profit by roughly 47%.
2. **Fix or reprice Tables and Bookcases.** Review supplier cost, shipping and discount policy. If margins cannot reach break-even, reduce the range.
3. **Run a Central-region turnaround.** Start with Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania and Illinois: check discount depth and product mix against the West.
4. **Push high-margin lines** (Copiers, Accessories, Paper, Labels, Envelopes) in bundles and marketing to lift blended margin.
